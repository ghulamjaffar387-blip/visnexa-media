Privacy Policy.

How VISNEXA Media handles information across our website, communications and authorized content-management integrations.

Effective date: October 13, 2026

VISNEXA Media is a digital content and affiliate product discovery brand. We respect your privacy. This policy describes our practices and the roles of third-party services we use.

1. Information we process

When you contact us, we may receive your name, email address and the information you choose to send. Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Hosting providers and linked third-party services may process technical information such as request logs and browser data under their own policies.

Our internal systems may access information from our own connected social-media accounts, such as profile details, authorized Pinterest boards and Pins, content metadata and performance information, subject to the permissions granted.

2. How we use information

  • Respond to messages, questions and collaboration enquiries.
  • Create, review, organize and publish VISNEXA Media content.
  • Manage our own authorized social-media accounts and related content.
  • Understand content performance, maintain security and improve operations.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Pinterest API integration

VISNEXA Media is developing an internal Pinterest content-management integration using n8n. If Pinterest grants access and we authorize the connection, it may read our own boards and Pins, publish human-approved Pins, organize visual content and retrieve available performance information.

Our intended application is for the VISNEXA Media account, not for collecting or managing third-party Pinterest users' data. We aim to use only the permissions needed and to follow Pinterest's API terms and developer policies.

4. Affiliate links

Some VISNEXA Media content contains affiliate links to merchants. If you make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a commission without additional cost to you. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Linked merchants are responsible for their own sites, transactions and privacy practices.

5. Third-party services

Our content operations use or may integrate services such as GitHub Pages, Google Sheets, n8n, Pinterest, Instagram, YouTube and Amazon Associates. We may also maintain a Google Sites version of our public content. These providers have their own privacy policies and may process information as independent service providers or controllers, depending on their roles.

6. Storage, access and retention

We aim to limit access to authorized personnel and necessary systems, protect credentials, and retain operational information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described or applicable requirements. We take reasonable precautions, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Cookies and external links

This static website does not intentionally set first-party analytics or advertising cookies. Hosting infrastructure and external services may use logs or cookies according to their own practices. Links to other websites are provided for convenience, and their privacy notices apply when you visit them.

8. Privacy requests

You may contact us to ask about information we control or to request access, correction or deletion where applicable. We will evaluate requests in accordance with applicable obligations.

9. Children's privacy

This website is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, please contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this notice when our services or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page, with a revised effective date where appropriate.
